# Research brief

Question: Find and approve 50 Vietnam innovative high-tech startups, prioritize physical products, using recent fund/government/contest publications, official tax status and alternative activity evidence.
Date of research: 2026-10-13
Effort tier: deep
Audience / use of the answer: NACENTECH; build a research and sourcing database, not an investment recommendation.
Scope: Vietnam legal entities; discovery publications in prior 730 days, activity in prior 365 days, tax lookup in prior 30 days. Rolling thresholds are project policy. Physical products are preferred, not compulsory.
Output format: reproducible tool, evidence ledger, screening table, 50 approved companies and Sheet-ready data.

## Sub-questions (acceptance tests — the report must give each a verdict)
- Q1: Can recent fund, government and contest publications yield a sufficiently broad candidate pool?
- Q2: Does each selected brand map to one Vietnam legal entity and parent tax ID?
- Q3: Does a recent official tax result state the entity is active?
- Q4: Do two independent recent activity signals support operation, after counter-evidence review? [contested]
- Q5: Does each company have a demonstrable innovative/high-tech product, with physical classification reviewed?
- Q6: Can all gates be satisfied for 50 unique companies, without filling gaps with assumptions?

## Search plan
- Broad queries first, then narrow. Note provider per query (exa / websearch / agent).

## Stop rule
Stop when every sub-question has: >=2 independent sources (>=1 tier 1-2) agreeing, OR an explicit
"contested" / "unknown after N queries" verdict, OR the tier budget is spent.
